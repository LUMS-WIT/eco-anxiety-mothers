# Eco-Anxiety in Perinatal Mothers (research prototype)

**Open the page:** https://lums-wit.github.io/eco-anxiety-mothers/

A one-page demonstration of an AI agent that estimates how likely mothers in different situations are to screen
positive for depression and anxiety in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, and explains why. It weighs water supply, flooding,
income and schooling, using lessons it learned from interviews and published research.

- **Recorded, not live.** The agent's answers were computed on our research laptop on 16 Nov 2026 (Qwen 3.5 9B,
  run locally; 5 versions of the agent) for 27 invented mothers. The page replays those recordings. Nothing
  runs on a server, and nothing you click is sent anywhere.
- **Group results only.** The survey figures are group results from 112 mothers interviewed by phone,
  23 Sep – 30 Oct 2026. No individual's answers are included.
- **Not a screening tool.** It gives likelihoods for groups of similar mothers. It cannot diagnose anyone.

The page is one self-contained file: `index.html`.
